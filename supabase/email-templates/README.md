# Vantage email templates

Branded to match the Clear Horizon design. To use them, open the Supabase
Dashboard, go to Authentication, then Email Templates:

1. **Invite user**: set the subject to `You're invited to Vantage` and paste in `invite.html`.
2. **Reset password**: set the subject to `Reset your password` and paste in `recovery.html`.

They are English only for now. The AZ and RU versions should be written and
reviewed by a native speaker before they are added.
